# Krebs cycle process lecture
has_process: true

Cellular respiration converts glucose into usable energy. First, glycolysis breaks glucose into pyruvate in the cytoplasm. Then the Krebs cycle oxidizes pyruvate-derived acetyl-CoA in the mitochondrial matrix. Finally, the electron transport chain uses the resulting NADH and FADH2 to make ATP.

Students often confuse the Krebs cycle with glycolysis. Glycolysis happens first in the cytoplasm. The Krebs cycle happens next inside mitochondria. Electron transport is last and produces most of the ATP.
