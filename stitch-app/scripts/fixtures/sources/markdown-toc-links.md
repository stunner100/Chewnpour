### Light reactions

The light reactions take place in the thylakoid membranes. Chlorophyll absorbs light and splits water, releasing oxygen. ATP and NADPH carry the captured energy to the next stage.

### Calvin cycle

The Calvin cycle runs in the stroma. The enzyme RuBisCO fixes carbon dioxide onto ribulose bisphosphate. ATP and NADPH from the light reactions turn the product into sugar.
