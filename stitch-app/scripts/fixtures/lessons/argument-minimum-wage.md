## Why This Matters

Minimum wage debates come up in every economics exam because they test supply, demand and evidence together.

## The Claim

Raising the minimum wage reduces poverty without large job losses.

## Evidence from the Source

Card and Krueger compared fast-food employment in New Jersey and Pennsylvania after New Jersey raised its wage in 1992. Employment did not fall in New Jersey relative to Pennsylvania.

## The Counterargument

Standard supply and demand predicts that if the wage floor w > the market wage, employers hire fewer workers. Critics also note the study covered one industry over a short period.

## How to Decide

Weigh the size of the increase against local wages: a small rise relative to the median wage is less likely to cut jobs than a large one.

## Recap from Memory

Name the study the source uses and state what it found in one sentence.

## Word Bank

- **Minimum wage**: the lowest hourly pay an employer may legally offer
- **Wage floor**: a price floor applied to the labour market
- **Natural experiment**: a comparison created by a real policy change rather than a lab
