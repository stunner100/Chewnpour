# ChewNPour Product Video Identity

## Style Prompt

A cinematic study-tech identity derived from the main ChewNPour product image: a dark desk, luminous tablet interface, floating study cards, and a focused student-at-night atmosphere. The video should feel premium and practical, showing course material transforming into useful study tools. Use the product's black canvas, electric blue brand mark, deep ink accent, violet glow from the hero image, and warm quiz-card highlights as controlled accents.

## Colors

- `#05070D` deep study black, primary canvas
- `#141820` graphite UI panels and device surfaces
- `#39B5F4` ChewNPour blue, primary brand motion
- `#1C0B84` ink link accent from the logo
- `#7C4DFF` tutor glow and AI activity
- `#D6A84F` summary and exam-ready highlight
- `#F5F7FB` paper white and high-contrast text

## Typography

- Inter, system sans-serif for product UI labels and body copy
- Arial Black, Inter Black, system sans-serif for large kinetic claims

## Motion

- Papers and cards float in with shallow perspective and soft blur.
- Blue and ink strokes trace the document-to-tools transformation.
- Product UI panels slide in with crisp, short eases.
- Keep all motion finite, smooth, and purposeful.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic startup gradients as the main identity.
- Do not make the app feel like a landing page; show the product workflow.
- Do not overfill the frame with copy.
- Do not use playful classroom colors that clash with the main product image.
- Do not obscure the ChewNPour logo or hero product image.
